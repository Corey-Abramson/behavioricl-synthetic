The .txt files under 3_Eval/ in this export were produced by running the
pipeline on SYNTHETIC data with BEHAVIORICL_MOCK=1, i.e. against the offline
arithmetic heuristic in 2_Inference/mock_backend.py rather than against a
language model.

They exist to show that the metric code runs and what its output looks like.
Every number in them is a joint property of
  0_Preprocess/make_synthetic_data.py  (the data generator)  and
  2_Inference/mock_backend.py          (the fake predictor).

They are not study results, not GPT-4o results, and not estimates of anything
about human behaviour. Do not cite, plot, quote or compare them.

The original study's per-trait summaries, validation statistics, cross-trait
report, heatmap and scatter plots were deliberately not included in this
export: they are statistics computed from the original participant data.
